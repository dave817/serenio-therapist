    What Yuyu is like

    Not a chirpy chatbot. A calm, thoughtful friend.

    Yuyu stays with the moment instead of rushing to fix it. Three things guide every reply.

    1. 01

      Reflects first, advises second

      Before any suggestion, Yuyu says back what it heard, so you feel understood rather than handled. Emotional accuracy, not generic comfort.

    2. 02

      Remembers what matters

      The people, hopes and worries you share carry from one conversation to the next. You can see, edit or forget any of it.

    3. 03

      Cares, honestly

      When something feels too heavy to hold alone, Yuyu gently points you toward people who can truly be there, with support lines one tap away.

    Inside Serenio

    Everything you need to set a feeling down, and nothing you don't.

    Talk it through

    Say it however it comes out.

    Type, speak or share a photo. Yuyu replies in your language and picks up the thread from last time, so you never have to start from the beginning.

    • Help writing a hard message, sincere and calm, not needy or sharp
    • Read the room: Yuyu reads your message as the other person might
    • In Traditional Chinese, chat in written Chinese or spoken Cantonese

    Something to try together

    Small practices, when you want them.

    Each practice is a short, guided conversation adapted from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and related approaches. Pick whatever fits the moment, and stop any time.

    • Ground me
    • Breathe with me
    • Untangle a thought
    • What if the worst happens?
    • One small step
    • Worry time
    • A kinder voice
    • What matters to me
    Where these practices come from

    Journal

    A journal that notices with you.

    Write, speak or add a photo. Each day brings a question drawn from your own pages, and Yuyu reflects back what it notices, with one gentle question to take further.

    • Your week in reflection, gathered into a few honest lines
    • Search your pages by mood, or by written, spoken and photo entries
    • Delete a page and what Yuyu learned from it goes too

    Insights

    Patterns worth noticing.

    Four weeks of your own days: how the weeks have felt, the thinking traps that keep appearing, what seemed to help, and what your journal returns to.

    • Built only from your own conversations, check-ins and journal
    • Patterns, not proof: what seemed to help is a pattern, never a promise
    • Not enough history yet? The space stays honestly blank

    Your memory, your choice

    You decide what Yuyu keeps.

    Start with a note, in your own words, about what Yuyu should know. Everything else it remembers sits in one place, grouped into people, hopes, what helps and more. Nothing is hidden from you.

    • Edit or forget any memory, or pause new ones
    • Download everything, any time
    • Delete your account, and all of it, from inside the app

    And a little more

    The standard PHQ-9 and GAD-7 questions, with plain-language results you can follow over time. A snapshot, never a diagnosis.

    A letter to your future self

    Seal a note for a week or up to a year. When it opens, Yuyu adds how much has happened since, without ever reading your words.

    How it works

    Built with care, and honest about its limits.

    An AI companion should be open about how it works. Here is the short version.

    • Safety first, on every message

      Crisis language is checked before Yuyu writes a word, in English, Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean. A second AI check looks for quieter signs.

    • Methods with a track record

      Practices adapted from CBT, a safety plan based on Stanley and Brown, and the standard PHQ-9 and GAD-7 questions.

    • Private by default

      No ads and no selling your data. Microsoft and OpenAI, whose models power Yuyu, don't train on your conversations.

    • Clear about what it isn't

      Yuyu is an AI, not a person and not a therapist. It doesn't diagnose, and it isn't an emergency service.

    A note from Dave

    Most of us are carrying more than we let on.

    I'm Dave, and Serenio began with helplessness. I watched my sister live with depression, and there were days I sat beside her hospital bed with everything I had to give and still no idea how to reach her. Caring about someone deeply, I learned, is not the same as knowing how to help them.

    That helplessness is why Serenio exists. I don't believe emotional support should be a luxury, or something you only reach for once things have already broken. So we built something gentle and always there: a companion that listens the moment a feeling stirs, sits with it, and helps you find your own words for it. And we made it free for anyone who needs it.

    A while after we launched, a man in his forties wrote to tell me Serenio had stayed with him through one of the lowest points of his life. Six months later he messaged again, just to say he was still here. I don't take a message like that lightly. It is the whole reason we keep going.

    I get to do this with people I trust, psychologists, engineers and human-computer researchers across Hong Kong, Denmark, Canada and Taiwan, with the support of the Technical University of Denmark, Accelerace, Micro Grant and Beyond Beta. We're small on purpose, and we care about the human part before anything else.

    Common questions

    Is Serenio really free?

    Yes, for individuals. There's no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising, on iPhone or on the web. To keep it sustainable, Yuyu gently closes the day after 50 replies, and replies during a hard moment never count toward that. Organizations that want to offer Serenio to their people work with us on a paid partnership.

    Is Yuyu a real person?

    No. Yuyu is an AI, software built on large language models. It's designed to listen carefully and reply warmly, but no person is typing on the other side, and it isn't a therapist.

    Is Serenio a replacement for therapy?

    No. Serenio is a companion for everyday feelings. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it isn't a medical device. For diagnosis or treatment, please speak with a qualified professional.

    What happens if I'm in crisis?

    Every message is checked for signs of crisis, and when there are signs of risk, Yuyu shows 24-hour support lines you can call with one tap. But Serenio is not an emergency service, and no one monitors conversations. If you are in danger, call 999 in Hong Kong or your local emergency number. You can also find support lines at any time on Serenio's support page, no account needed.

    Are my conversations private?

    Your conversations belong to your account. We don't sell data or show ads, and Microsoft and OpenAI, whose models power Yuyu, don't use your conversations to train their models. Journal videos are made by Volcengine (ByteDance) in mainland China, and only if you ask for one. You can download everything, or delete your account, at any time. See where your words go.

    Who can use Serenio?

    Adults aged 18 and over. The iPhone app needs iOS 18 or later and is available in App Stores across Asia-Pacific. The web app works anywhere, in any modern browser, including on Android.

    Which languages does Yuyu speak?

    English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Yuyu replies in the language you write in, and in Traditional Chinese you can choose written Chinese or spoken Cantonese.
