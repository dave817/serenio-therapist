    Privacy Policy

    How we collect, use, share and protect your personal data, in plain words.

    In short

    • We use your data to run Serenio for you: to reply to you, keep your history, and remember what you have chosen to let Yuyu remember.
    • Yuyu is an AI. To reply, your messages and related context are processed by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service and OpenAI, which do not use your data to train their models.
    • We don't sell your personal data, and we don't show ads.
    • You can see and edit what Yuyu remembers, download your data and delete your account from inside the app.
    • Serenio is not an emergency service, and no one monitors your conversations.

    1. Who we are

    Serenio AI Limited ("Serenio", "we", "us") is a company incorporated in Hong Kong (company no. 3299059). We provide the Serenio app for iPhone, the Serenio web app at webapp.serenio.ai and this website at serenio.ai (together, the "Services"). We decide how and why your personal data is processed, which makes us the data user (or "controller") under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) and similar laws. On the App Store, the Serenio app may be listed under our founder's developer account; this policy applies to it in exactly the same way.

    2. What we collect

    Information you give us

    • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider.
    • Profile: the name you'd like Yuyu to call you, your age range, language, time zone, an optional note about yourself, and your reminder preferences.
    • What you share and create: your messages to Yuyu; journal pages (writing, voice notes and photos), with moods and tags; mood check-ins; letters and time-capsule letters; your safety plan; your answers to the check-in questionnaires (PHQ-9 and GAD-7) and their results; the practices you use; and any summaries you choose to share.
    • Photos: images you add to a chat or your journal. We remove location data from photos when you upload them.
    • Voice: when you speak instead of typing, your recording is sent for transcription. Recordings made in chat are not kept after they are transcribed. Voice notes you save in your journal are kept as part of your journal until you delete them.
    • Messages to us: what you send by email or through our contact form.

    Information created as you use Serenio

    • Yuyu's replies, and what Serenio derives from your content to be helpful: short conversation summaries; memories (for example, the people you mention, your hopes and what helps you); emotion notes (for example, "calm" or "heavy") with an estimate of intensity; topics; reflections; and insights.
    • Technical information needed to run and secure the Services, such as IP address, device and browser type, and timestamps, held in service logs. When the safety checks detect risk, we log only a category code, never your words.

    Sensitive information

    What you share with Serenio may reveal information about your health, including your mental health, and other sensitive matters. We use it only to provide the Services to you. You choose what to share, and you can delete it at any time.

    3. How we use your data

    We use your personal data to:

    • provide the Services: write Yuyu's replies, keep your history across sessions and devices, remember what you have let Yuyu remember, and create reflections, insights, letters and check-in results;
    • keep you safe: check messages for signs of crisis and show support resources;
    • send the reminders you ask for;
    • secure, maintain, fix and improve the Services, using de-identified or aggregated information wherever we can;
    • reply when you contact us; and
    • meet our legal obligations.

    We don't use your conversations for advertising, we don't sell your personal data, and we don't make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing.

    We may create de-identified or aggregated statistics, such as how many people use a feature. These don't identify you, and we may share them, including with academic partners. We will not share your conversations or journal, even de-identified, without your consent.

    If you are in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the UK, we rely on these legal bases: our contract with you (to provide the Services); your explicit consent for health information you choose to share (which you can withdraw by deleting it or your account); our legitimate interests in keeping the Services secure and improving them; and our legal obligations.

    4. How the AI uses your data

    • Replies. When you send a message, we send it, together with the context Yuyu needs to reply, to Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service, or to OpenAI if Azure is unavailable. That context can include the name you'd like to be called, your age range, your note about yourself, recent conversation summaries, relevant memories, recent practices and, during a support moment, parts of your safety plan. Your email address is not sent.
    • Background work. We use OpenAI models to check messages for risk, note emotions, pick out memories, summarise conversations, write journal reflections, daily questions, insights, letters and check-in summaries, describe and check photos, build search indexes for your memories, and transcribe voice. Voice input in the iPhone app is transcribed by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service.
    • No model training. Microsoft and OpenAI process this data under their business terms, under which they do not use it to train their models. They may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, and then delete it.
    • Journal videos. If you ask Serenio to paint a day as a short video, a text description that includes up to 600 characters of your most recent conversation is sent to Volcengine (a ByteDance company), which creates the video on servers in mainland China. We send no account details, such as your email address, but the conversation excerpt itself may contain personal information. If you never create a video, nothing is sent to Volcengine.
    • iPhone voice conversations. In voice mode, the iPhone app uses Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your language and device, this happens on your iPhone or on Apple's servers (Cantonese uses Apple's servers). Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing. Replies read aloud use your device's built-in voice.
    • AI can be wrong. Yuyu's replies, reflections and insights are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or incomplete.

    6. International transfers

    Serenio is based in Hong Kong, but our providers process data elsewhere, including in India, the United States, wherever Microsoft Azure runs the Azure OpenAI Service for us and, for journal videos only, mainland China. These places may have data-protection laws that differ from yours. We choose providers with strong security commitments and rely on contractual safeguards when your data moves across borders.

    7. How long we keep it

    • We keep your account and content until you delete them or your account.
    • Journal pages: after you delete a page, you have about ten minutes to undo. It is then permanently removed, together with what Yuyu learned from it.
    • Memories: when you forget a memory, it is removed, including from conversation summaries.
    • Conversations: individual conversations can't yet be deleted one by one. Deleting your account deletes all of them.
    • Shared summary links expire after 30 days, and you can revoke them sooner.
    • Chat voice recordings are not kept after transcription.
    • Deleting your account removes your stored files, then your account and everything linked to it, and we check that nothing is left. Copies in our providers' routine backups disappear as those backups expire.
    • Service logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.
    • iPhone versions without accounts, and earlier versions: versions of the iPhone app that don't ask you to sign in (before version 1.1) keep your conversations and journal on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes them. Messages, voice for transcription and any journal-video requests are still processed by the providers described in this policy so that Yuyu can reply. Data from those versions, and from any earlier version of Serenio that we hold, is not included in the in-app download or deletion: email us and we will send it to you or delete it.

    8. How we protect it

    • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.
    • Database rules mean each account can access only its own records.
    • Photos, voice notes and videos are kept in private storage and shown only through links that expire.
    • Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it to run Serenio.

    No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

    9. Your rights and choices

    Wherever you live, you can:

    • see, correct and delete your data: edit your profile, review, edit or forget memories, and delete journal pages;
    • download your data with "Download everything" in the You tab;
    • delete your account and all of its data with "Delete my account" in the You tab;
    • pause new memories with "Remember new things";
    • withdraw consent, by deleting the content concerned or your account; and
    • turn reminders on or off.

    Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may also have the right to request access to and correction of your data (in Hong Kong, under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance), to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To make a request, email support@serenio.ai. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time the law requires (for a data access request under the Ordinance, 40 days).

    If you have a concern, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data in Hong Kong or, if you are in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, to your local data protection authority.

    10. Children

    Serenio is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using Serenio, please contact us and we will delete the account.

    11. This website: cookies and analytics

    This website (serenio.ai) uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages help them. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the website does not load Google Analytics at all. Advertising features are turned off for everyone, and visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are counted without analytics cookies. The website also remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage.

    The Serenio apps don't contain advertising or analytics trackers. The iPhone app sends reminders only if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

    12. Support lines and other services

    Serenio shows helplines and links to other services. When you contact a helpline or visit another website, that organization's own terms and privacy policy apply.

    13. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date and, if the changes are significant, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.

    14. Contact us

    Serenio AI Limited (Hong Kong company no. 3299059). For privacy questions, requests and complaints, email support@serenio.ai.

    1. 我們是誰

    Serenio AI Limited（思癒科技有限公司，下稱「Serenio」或「我們」）是一家於香港註冊成立的公司（公司編號 3299059）。我們提供 Serenio iPhone App、位於 webapp.serenio.ai 的 Serenio 網頁版，以及位於 serenio.ai 的本網站（統稱「本服務」）。由我們決定如何及為何處理你的個人資料，因此根據香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）及同類法律，我們是「資料使用者」（或「控制者」）。在 App Store 上，Serenio App 可能以我們創辦人的開發者帳戶上架；本政策同樣適用。

    2. 我們收集甚麼

    你提供給我們的資料

    • 帳戶：你的電郵地址及密碼。密碼由我們的登入服務供應商以雜湊形式儲存。
    • 個人檔案：你希望小癒稱呼你的名字、年齡範圍、語言、時區、一段關於你自己的選填簡介，以及提醒設定。
    • 你分享及建立的內容：你傳給小癒的訊息；日記頁面（文字、語音筆記及相片），連同心情與標籤；心情簽到；信件及時光膠囊信；你的安全計劃；你在檢視問卷（PHQ-9 及 GAD-7）中的答案及結果；你使用過的練習；以及你選擇分享的任何摘要。
    • 相片：你在對話或日記中加入的圖片。上載時，我們會移除相片中的位置資料。
    • 語音：當你用說話代替打字，你的錄音會被傳送作轉寫。在對話中錄製的錄音，轉寫後不會保留；你存入日記的語音筆記，會作為日記的一部分保存，直至你刪除為止。
    • 給我們的訊息：你透過電郵或聯絡表格傳給我們的內容。

    你使用 Serenio 時產生的資料

    • 小癒的回覆，以及 Serenio 為了幫上忙而從你的內容整理出的資料：簡短的對話摘要；記憶（例如你提到的人、你的期望、對你有幫助的事）；情緒紀錄（例如「平靜」或「沉重」）及強度估計；話題；回應與觀察。
    • 運作及保護本服務所需的技術資料，例如 IP 地址、裝置與瀏覽器類型及時間戳記，儲存在服務紀錄中。當安全檢查偵測到風險時，我們只記錄類別代碼，絕不記錄你的原文。

    敏感資料

    你與 Serenio 分享的內容，可能透露你的健康狀況（包括心理健康）及其他敏感事項。我們只會用它來為你提供本服務。分享甚麼由你決定，你亦可隨時刪除。

    3. 我們如何使用你的資料

    我們使用你的個人資料來：

    • 提供本服務：撰寫小癒的回覆、跨對話及跨裝置保存你的紀錄、記住你允許小癒記住的事，以及產生回應、觀察、信件及檢視結果；

    • 履行我們的法律責任。

    我們不會將你的對話用於廣告，不會出售你的個人資料，亦不會純粹以自動化處理，作出對你有法律效果或同等重大影響的決定。

    4. AI 如何使用你的資料

    • 回覆。當你傳送訊息，我們會把訊息連同小癒回覆所需的脈絡，傳送至 Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service；如 Azure 無法使用，則改為傳送至 OpenAI。脈絡可能包括你希望被稱呼的名字、年齡範圍、你的自我簡介、近期對話摘要、相關記憶、近期練習，以及在支援時刻中你安全計劃的部分內容。你的電郵地址不會被傳送。
    • 背景工作。我們使用 OpenAI 的模型來檢查訊息的風險、記錄情緒、整理記憶、摘要對話、撰寫日記回應、每日問題、觀察、信件及檢視摘要、描述及檢查相片、為你的記憶建立搜尋索引，以及轉寫語音。iPhone App 的語音輸入則由 Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service 轉寫。
    • 不用於訓練模型。Microsoft 及 OpenAI 按其企業條款處理這些資料；根據這些條款，它們不會以此訓練模型。它們可能會為偵測濫用而保留資料最多 30 天，之後便會刪除。
    • 日記影片。如你請 Serenio 把某一天畫成短片，一段包含你最近對話最多 600 個字元的文字描述，會傳送至火山引擎（Volcengine，字節跳動旗下公司），由其位於中國內地的伺服器製作影片。我們不會傳送任何帳戶資料（例如你的電郵地址），但對話節錄本身可能包含個人資料。如你從未製作影片，便不會有任何資料傳送至火山引擎。
    • iPhone 語音對話。在語音模式下，iPhone App 使用 Apple 的語音辨識。視乎語言及裝置，辨識會在你的 iPhone 上或 Apple 的伺服器上進行（廣東話會使用 Apple 的伺服器），並受 Apple 的私隱政策規管。朗讀回覆時，使用的是你裝置內置的語音。
    • AI 可能出錯。小癒的回覆、回應及觀察均由系統自動產生，可能不準確或不完整。

    7. 我們保存多久

    • 我們會保存你的帳戶及內容，直至你刪除它們或你的帳戶。
    • 日記頁面：刪除後約十分鐘內可以復原，之後會被永久移除，小癒從該頁學到的內容亦會一併移除。
    • 記憶：當你忘掉一則記憶，它會被移除，包括在對話摘要中。
    • 對話：目前未能逐段刪除個別對話；刪除帳戶會刪除所有對話。
    • 分享摘要的連結會在 30 天後失效，你亦可提早撤回。
    • 對話中的語音錄音轉寫後不會保留。
    • 刪除帳戶時，我們會先移除你儲存的檔案，再刪除帳戶及所有相關內容，並確認沒有任何殘留。供應商例行備份中的副本，會隨備份到期而消失。
    • 服務紀錄會為保安及排解問題而保存一段有限時間。
    • 無需帳戶的 iPhone 版本及舊版本：不要求你登入的 iPhone App 版本（1.1 版之前）會把你的對話和日記保存在你的 iPhone 上，刪除 App 即會移除。為了讓小癒回覆，訊息、需要轉寫的語音及日記影片請求，仍會交由本政策所述的供應商處理。這些版本及任何更早版本的 Serenio 中由我們保存的資料，並不包括在 App 內的下載或刪除功能中；請電郵給我們，我們會把資料提供給你或刪除。

    8. 我們如何保護資料

    • 資料在傳輸時加密（HTTPS/TLS），並由我們的基礎設施供應商在儲存時加密。
    • 資料庫規則確保每個帳戶只能存取自己的紀錄。
