    For organizations

    Bring Serenio to the people in your care.

    Serenio is free for individuals. When a workplace, university or care organization wants to offer it to its people, we work together on a paid partnership.

    For individuals

    Free

    On iPhone and the web, for anyone who needs it. No subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

    For organizations

    Paid partnership

    For workplaces, universities and colleges, care organizations and NGOs that want to offer Serenio to the adults in their care. We shape it with you and agree the cost together.

    Why Serenio

    Everyday support, alongside the care people already have.

    • There in the in-between hours

      Feelings don't keep office hours. Yuyu is there after work, at 2 a.m. or on a Sunday, in four languages, including chat in spoken Cantonese.

    • Built to point toward people

      When things get heavy, Yuyu surfaces 24-hour support lines and each person's own safety plan, and encourages them to reach real people. It complements professional care; it never replaces it.

    • Privacy people can trust

      Each person's conversations are private to them. We never share what anyone says to Yuyu with their employer, university or organization.

    Questions from organizations

    Is Serenio free for organizations?

    No. Serenio is free for individuals. When an organization offers Serenio to its people, that's a paid partnership. Get in touch and we'll talk it through.

    Can we see what our people say to Yuyu?

    No. Conversations belong to each person. We don't share them with employers, universities or any organization.

    Does Serenio replace counselling or an employee assistance programme?

    No. Serenio is a companion for everyday feelings, not a clinical service. It works alongside professional support and points people toward it.

    How does Serenio handle safety?

    Every message is checked for signs of crisis, and people are shown 24-hour support lines they can call with a tap. Serenio isn't an emergency service, and no one monitors conversations. Read exactly what happens.

    Which languages are supported?

    English, Traditional Chinese (including chat in spoken Cantonese), Japanese and Korean.

    Who is behind Serenio?

    Serenio AI Limited, a Hong Kong company (no. 3299059) founded by Dave in 2023, with a team across Hong Kong, Denmark, Canada and Taiwan.
